This policy explains how the operator of Split handles information when using this Python-based content assistant.

3. Authentication Tokens

OAuth access tokens and refresh tokens are sensitive credentials. They should be stored securely, should not be shared publicly, and should only be used for the purposes authorized by the relevant platform.

4. Content and Local Processing

Split is designed to run as a Python application on the operator's computer. Depending on its implementation, account information, tokens, video files, and logs may be processed or stored locally. Do not use this statement as a guarantee until the actual application behavior has been verified.

5. Sharing and Third Parties

Information sent to a social media platform is handled according to that platform's policies. The operator should not disclose personal information to other parties unless necessary, lawful, and appropriately disclosed.

6. Data Retention and Deletion

Information should be retained only for as long as necessary for the application's stated purposes. Users may contact the operator to ask about applicable access or deletion requests.

7. Security

Reasonable measures should be taken to protect account information and authentication credentials. No method of storage or transmission is completely secure.

This policy may be updated when the application's functionality or data practices change.
